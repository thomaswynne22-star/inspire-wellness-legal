Privacy Policy

Last updated September 13, 2026

What this app is

Inspire Wellness STL ("we," "us," "the App") is a wellness quiz and content app operated by Inspire Wellness STL, based in St. Louis, Missouri. The App is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition — it is not a healthcare provider, and nothing in the App constitutes medical advice.

Information we collect

We do not require you to create an account, and we never ask for your name, email, or password to use the quizzes. The App uses anonymous, device-based sign-in — you get a random identifier tied to your device/browser, not to your identity.

Specifically, we collect:

  • Quiz answers. Your goal, dietary preferences and restrictions, health focus areas, meal-prep time preference, and what foods you enjoy — used to build your 3-day meal plan, a simple daily movement suggestion, and a supplement match. We do not ask your age anywhere in the App today.
  • Generated plans. The 3-day meal plan the App builds for you, saved so you can return to it later and so it syncs across your devices if you use the App on more than one.
  • Basic technical data. Your browser's user agent and the page that referred you to the App, logged when you open the App, plus a record of when you click through to a recipe or product link on our partner store. This is used only in aggregate, to understand how the App is used — it is not shown to other visitors and is not sold.
  • Purchase data, if you buy something. If you click through to our store and complete a purchase there, we may receive order information (order number, amount, items) from our store platform so we can measure which in-app recommendations led to a sale. We do not process payments in the App and never see your payment card details — purchases happen entirely on our store's own checkout.

We do not currently collect or respond to browser "Do Not Track" signals — the App does not use third-party advertising trackers, so there is presently nothing for such a signal to turn off.

What we don't collect

We don't collect your name, email address, phone number, or physical address unless you choose to give it to us some other way (e.g., contacting support). We don't collect payment information. We don't use the App's quiz data to build an advertising profile, and we do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Categories of third parties we share data with

  • Anthropic (AI provider) — receives your quiz answers only, to generate your plan. Does not receive your name or any other identifying information.
  • Supabase (database/hosting provider) — stores your quiz answers, saved plans, and the anonymous technical data described above, under the access controls described below.
  • Our store platform — if you make a purchase there, its own systems (not this App) handle your order and payment. See the third-party site's own privacy policy for how it handles that data.
  • Google Fonts, unpkg.com, and jsDelivr (content delivery networks) — deliver the App's web fonts, icon set, and the Supabase code library itself. Like any website that uses a CDN, these receive basic technical request data (e.g., your IP address) as an inherent part of loading those files — they don't receive your quiz answers, plans, or any other information from the App.

We do not otherwise sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their own use.

How we use AI to generate your plan

Your quiz answers (not your name or any identifying information) may be sent to a third-party AI provider (Anthropic) to generate your personalized meal plan. If that service is unavailable, the App falls back to a plan built from pre-written templates on our own servers — either way, your plan is generated using only the answers you gave in that quiz session.

Where your data is stored, and how it's protected

Your quiz answers and saved plans are stored using Supabase, our database provider, behind row-level access controls that ensure your data is only ever readable by your own anonymous session — not by other visitors, and not by us browsing a raw table. Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS); Supabase encrypts data at rest. Some data is also cached in your browser's local storage for the App to work offline or before you're synced.

Your rights and choices

  • You can retake either quiz at any time, which replaces your previous answers.
  • Saved plans can be viewed and replaced from the App's "View saved plans" section.
  • Press "Delete my data" from the gear-icon Settings menu (or "Clear my data" from "View saved plans") to permanently delete your saved plans, your anonymous account, and your app-visit and link-click history from our servers immediately — no account, email, or charge required. We retain only the bare fact that you consented to our Terms of Service — the date and which version you agreed to, with no other data attached to it — solely as our own record in case that consent is ever disputed, the same reason any business keeps a copy of a signed agreement. The same request also works from a standalone page if you'd rather not open the App to do it, as long as it's opened on the same device/browser you used the App on.
  • If you are a California resident, you may also have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (as amended by the CPRA), including the right to know what personal information we hold about you, the right to request its deletion, and the right to opt out of its sale or sharing — we do not sell or share personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of today, but you can still exercise the other rights through the contact method below. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

Children's privacy

This App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you're a parent or guardian and believe your child has given us information without your consent, reach us through inspirewellnessstl.com and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App changes. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above; if a change would materially expand how we use information we already hold about you, we will provide more prominent notice before it takes effect.

Dispute resolution
